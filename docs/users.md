# User onboarding evidence (Level 4)

Track **10+ real users** who connected a wallet and completed at least one interaction on the live app.

**Live app:** https://stellarchatpay.vercel.app/

## How to collect proof

1. Share the live link with testers (RiseIn cohort, friends, Discord).
2. Ask each person to: **Connect wallet** → `fund` (if needed) → `balance` → `send 1 to G…` (or escrow).
3. Record their **truncated address**, **action**, and **Stellar Expert tx link**.
4. Optionally ask them to open the feedback button (chat header) and leave a rating.

## Tips

- Distinct wallets count — not the same account 10 times.
- Albedo works well for mobile testers.
- Keep screenshots of the activity feed or success cards if useful for the demo video.
